WHY IT MATTERS

Research earlier.
Build with more context.

Developing a product takes time, money and countless decisions. Patent publications can reveal earlier technical approaches, unfamiliar terminology and ideas worth investigating before you commit to a design.

But finding a similar title is not enough. You need to see what a source says, which features overlap, and where the search has gaps. PatentRadar keeps those questions close to your technical work.

Use it to explore a field, prepare a research brief or bring better questions to a patent professional.

AN HONEST STARTING POINT

Evidence helps.
Coverage still matters.

SPOT currently compares against a reference index of 18 real medical patent publications. Broader research uses separate providers with varying access and coverage.

An empty result does not mean no patent exists. Similarity does not determine patentability, infringement or freedom to operate. Claims, jurisdiction, legal status and expert review remain essential.
